# Visual style: obsidian-frost

Deep obsidian foundation, crystalline white typography, sub-zero glassmorphism, and chilled cyan-blue frost illumination. Designed for cinematic high-gravitas technical presentations, executive reviews, and mission-critical engineering.

---

## 1. Shape & decoration

- **Shape language**: Expansive rounded containers (`rounded-xl` and `rounded-2xl`, 24px-32px radius) paired with aerodynamic pill badges (`rounded-full`, 9999px radius). Etched borderlines with hairline strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Composition geometry**: Deep volcanic obsidian cards layered with ambient sub-zero fog, subtle radial frost gradients, asymmetric hero splits, and frosted glass telemetry panels (`rgba(20, 24, 32, 0.65)` with backdrop blur).
- **Decoration**: Icy cyan-blue glow accents (`#8bb4d9`, `#a2cbf1`), cold metallic silver dividers, uppercase micro-labels with wide tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.15em`), and watermark-style background typography.
- **Whitespace**: Expansive breathing room allowing imagery and telemetry to emerge from deep light-absorbing charcoal shadows.

## 2. Typography character

- **Title & Display**: `EB Garamond` with spacious letter-spacing and strict uppercase transformation for monumental headlines, section headers, and stat values.
- **Body & Telemetry**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` for narrative body copy, micro-labels, metrics descriptors, and normative parameters.
- **Micro-labels**: Strict uppercase formatting with `0.15em` letter spacing (`label-caps`).

## 3. Using the deck's colors

- **Background**: `#10141a` (Obsidian Base) / `#090b0e` (Abyss Base).
- **Surface**: `#1c2026` / elevated `#262a31` / glass `rgba(20, 24, 32, 0.65)`.
- **Primary Text**: `#ffffff` (Stark White) / `#dfe2eb` (On-surface).
- **Accent & Illumination**: `#a2cbf1` / `#8bb4d9` (Frost Icy Blue) and `#ffb4ab` / `#FF3366` (Thermal Alerts).
- **Outlines**: `#444748` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## 4. Texture / elevation

- Sub-zero glassmorphism and luminous frost bleeds rather than daylight drop shadows.
- Elevation marked by hairline top-edge highlights (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and exterior frost aura (`0 0 20px rgba(139, 180, 217, 0.18)`).

## 5. Paired image-rendering

`digital-dashboard`, `blueprint`, or `photo-editorial` with cold grading and sub-zero vignetting.

## 6. Illustration propensity

**restrained** — Frost telemetry badges, technical schemas, and 3D character hero cards lead with sharp contrast and frosted containment.
